Human Development Index

Machine Learning Based Prediction System

The Human Development Index (HDI) is a statistical measure developed by the United Nations to evaluate the overall development of countries based on three major dimensions: Life Expectancy, Education, and Gross National Income (GNI) per capita. This application uses a Machine Learning model to predict the Human Development Index based on user-provided values. Select a country, enter the required indicators, and instantly obtain the predicted HDI score.
